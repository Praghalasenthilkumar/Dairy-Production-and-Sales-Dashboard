# Dairy Production & Sales Dashboard

1. Project Title / Headline

**Dairy Insights: Production & Sales Performance Dashboard**

A four-page Power BI dashboard that tracks dairy production and sales across Indian states, drilling down from overall output to product-level revenue.

2. Short Description / Purpose

The Dairy Production & Sales Dashboard is an interactive Power BI report that helps users monitor how much dairy is produced, where it is sold, which products lead in volume, and how revenue flows across states and sales channels. It is intended for operations managers, distribution planners, and sales analysts who need a single view of production and sales performance.

3. Tech Stack

The dashboard was built using the following tools and technologies:

- **Power BI Desktop:** Main data visualization platform used for report creation.
- **Power Query:** Data transformation and cleaning layer for reshaping and preparing the data.
- **DAX (Data Analysis Expressions):** Used for calculated measures such as totals, revenue, and cost metrics.
- **Data Modeling:** Relationships among production, sales, and product tables to enable cross-filtering and aggregation.
- **File Format:** `.pbix` for development and `.png` for dashboard previews.

4. Data Source

Sales and production records covering multiple Indian states (including Bihar, Chandigarh, Delhi, Rajasthan, Uttar Pradesh, Karnataka, Tamil Nadu, Kerala, and others), with product-level detail for brands such as Amul, Mother Dairy, Raj, Sudha, and Dodla Dairy.

5. Features / Highlights

Business Problem

Dairy operations generate large volumes of production and sales data across many states, brands, and product lines. Without a consolidated view, it is hard for managers to see which states lead in output, which products drive volume, how sales channels perform over time, and where revenue actually comes from.

Goal of the Dashboard

- Monitor total dairy production and cow count by location.
- Compare sales volume, unit price, and revenue across states and products.
- Identify top-performing states and best-selling products.
- Track Online, Retail, and Wholesale channel performance month by month.

Walkthrough of Key Visuals

**Dairy Production page**
- **KPI cards:** Total production (2.17M), selected location, and cow count (238K).
- **Total Dairy Production trend:** Monthly line chart showing seasonal peaks and dips.
- **Production Quantity by Product Name:** Treemap comparing Curd, Milk, Lassi, Butter, Yogurt, Buttermilk, Cheese, Paneer, Ice Cream, and Ghee.
- **Top 3 Production States:** Donut chart comparing Chandigarh, Delhi, and Uttar Pradesh.
- **Drill-down table:** Location → Product → Brand hierarchy with quantity and unit price.

**Dairy Sales page**
- **KPI cards:** Total sales (1M), revenue (58.73M), and cost price (236.92K).
- **Sales by Channel:** Monthly trend of Online, Retail, and Wholesale sales.
- **Top 3 Selling States:** Pie chart of Chandigarh, Delhi, and Rajasthan.
- **Sales Quantity by Product Name:** Treemap of product-level sales volume.
- **Drill-down table:** Location → Product with quantity sold, unit price, and total revenue.

Slicers: Year, Location, Product Name, and Brand filter every page.

Business Impact & Insights

- **Production and sales leaders:** Chandigarh records the highest quantity sold (134,625 units) and total revenue (about 7.54 crore).
- **Product mix:** Curd, Butter, and Lassi dominate sales quantity, while Ghee and Ice Cream contribute smaller volumes.
- **Channel behavior:** Retail and Wholesale consistently outperform Online sales, with a visible peak around June.
- **Seasonality:** Production peaks in June and September and dips in July and October, which supports better production scheduling and forecasting.
- **Business value:** Helps managers spot states where production and sales are out of balance, guides inventory and procurement, and shows which channels to invest in.
